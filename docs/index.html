About the Project

ImmersiveDawgs is working on an XR project that teaches the user the more advanced side of volleyball. Specficically the advanced rotational system called "5-1", the most popular rotation among higher level volleyball.

This VR training app will help users improve their knowledge of volleyball by interacting with other users to maintain proper rotation while also optimizing individual's location on the court relative to their position.

This app will support multi-player interaction guided by AI guided NPCs. Each NPC will explain the responsibilities of each position on the court and how to rotate properly through the 5-1 system.
